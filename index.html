Straight answers

Before you apply.

The questions that come up most often, answered without the sales pitch.

Is this a signals group?

No. The terminal assembles a read and shows its working — agreement, dissent, risk and timing. What you do with it stays your decision, and the desk will happily tell you when there is nothing to do.

What do I need to run it?

A browser on desktop or mobile. The terminal is built to be read on a phone between sessions, not chained to six monitors.

Which markets does it cover?

Futures and indices, metals, major FX pairs and crypto majors. The session clock and risk gate apply to all of them.

What happens after I apply?

Your application is reviewed by hand. If it's a fit, a licence key tied to your email is issued and delivered on WhatsApp — no card details, no auto-renewal.
